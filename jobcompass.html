AI-Powered Career Platform for UK Tech Job Seekers

February 2026 – Present · Personal project

JobCompass helps UK tech job seekers discover relevant roles, score matches against their profile, and move through applications with safer automation. I designed it as a hybrid system: autonomous AI agents handle unpredictable external sites, while deterministic pipelines own auth, matching, tracking, and analytics.

View on GitHub Architecture blog

Problem

Job search across company career sites is fragmented. Listings change often, ATS forms differ by employer, and fully autonomous apply-bots create reliability and compliance risk. JobCompass focuses on assisted workflows that stay useful without hard-coding every site or collecting unnecessary sensitive data.

Architecture at a glance

Tasks that must adapt to external sites (scraping, form reading, research) run as agents. Predictable internal work (authentication, scoring materialisation, application tracking, analytics) stays in deterministic FastAPI services backed by PostgreSQL, Redis, and Celery.

  • Scraper agent: Playwright-based ingestion with per-domain rate limiting, circuit breakers, and change detection.
  • Application agent: human-in-the-loop ATS automation that reads forms at runtime—no hard-coded site mappings—with server-side thresholds, daily caps, and pause gates for low-confidence or sensitive fields.
  • Match pipeline: materialised scoring (semantic, keyword, and seniority weighted) with delta re-computation when new data arrives, instead of full-table scoring at query time.
  • Interview prep: LLM-based generator with a template fallback for reliability.
  • Auth & privacy: OAuth binding and phone verification instead of identity-verification approaches that risk GDPR special-category data.

Stack

Python, FastAPI, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Playwright
